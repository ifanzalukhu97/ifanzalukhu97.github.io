A hands-on builder who happens to lead engineering teams.

With 6+ years in C# and .NET, I design and ship scalable backend systems — from high-performance APIs to complex microservices architectures across SaaS and supply chain domains. As Head of Engineering at SimpliDOTS, I balance engineering strategy with staying deep in the code.
